<p class="text-xl text-secondary font-medium leading-relaxed mb-8">
India has emerged as an indisputable epicenter for global silicon engineering, with over 20% of the world's semiconductor design workforce based in tech hubs like Bengaluru, Noida, and Hyderabad. Yet for design center heads, assembling specialized teams for sub-5nm tape-outs remains an acute challenge.
</p>

<h2 class="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-heading font-bold text-primary mt-10 mb-4">The Talent Bottleneck in Modern Silicon Design</h2>

<p class="text-secondary leading-relaxed mb-6">
As tape-out cycles accelerate and multi-die chiplet architectures become standard, the demand for senior engineers with hands-on exposure to finFET and GAA (Gate-All-Around) technologies has outpaced supply. The talent shortage is particularly severe across three specialized sub-disciplines:
</p>

<div class="grid md:grid-cols-3 gap-6 my-8">
    <div class="bg-gray-50 border border-gray-200 p-6 rounded-2xl">
        <h3 class="text-base font-bold text-primary mb-2">Physical Design (PD)</h3>
        <p class="text-secondary text-xs leading-relaxed">
            Severe competition for leads skilled in synthesis, floorplanning, CTS, routing, and signoff timing closure at advanced nodes.
        </p>
    </div>
    <div class="bg-gray-50 border border-gray-200 p-6 rounded-2xl">
        <h3 class="text-base font-bold text-primary mb-2">Design Verification (DV)</h3>
        <p class="text-secondary text-xs leading-relaxed">
            High demand for UVM and SystemVerilog architects capable of building constrained-random verification testbenches for complex SoCs.
        </p>
    </div>
    <div class="bg-gray-50 border border-gray-200 p-6 rounded-2xl">
        <h3 class="text-base font-bold text-primary mb-2">Embedded Firmware</h3>
        <p class="text-secondary text-xs leading-relaxed">
            Shortage of low-level C/C++ developers who bridge hardware and software with deep board bring-up and RTOS driver experience.
        </p>
    </div>
</div>

<h2 class="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-heading font-bold text-primary mt-10 mb-4">Why Traditional Hiring Fails in VLSI</h2>

<p class="text-secondary leading-relaxed mb-6">
Unlike software engineering where automated coding tests can filter basic candidates, hardware engineering demands nuanced assessment. Standard automated resume filters frequently confuse high-level verification engineers with physical verification specialists, creating enormous frustration for hiring managers.
</p>

<p class="text-secondary leading-relaxed mb-6">
Successful silicon recruitment requires headhunters who understand EDA toolchains (Synopsys, Cadence, Siemens EDA), tape-out timelines, and specific architectural protocols like PCIe Gen 5/6, CXL, and AMBA AXI.
</p>

<div class="bg-orange-50 border-l-4 border-accent p-6 rounded-r-2xl my-8">
    <h3 class="text-lg font-bold text-primary mb-2">Gigworx Sourcing Insight</h3>
    <p class="text-secondary text-sm leading-relaxed">
        Over 85% of tier-1 ASIC and physical design engineers are passive talent not actively applying on public portals. Reaching them requires discrete, peer-level headhunting led by recruiter specialists who understand semiconductor workflows.
    </p>
</div>
